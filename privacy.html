Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Short version: Ninja Piano does not require an account and does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your settings and practice progress stay on your own device. We have no servers that receive your data.

What we store

The app saves your preferences and progress — such as your name label, chosen theme, tempo, volume, and quiz scores — in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes this information.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, logins, or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data — we don't collect any to begin with.
  • No location, contacts, or device-identifier collection.

Fonts

The app loads a typeface from Google Fonts. When it does, your device requests the font file from Google's servers, which means your IP address is visible to Google for that request, as with any website that uses Google Fonts. See Google's privacy policy.

Scan sheet music (optional)

The app includes an optional "Scan sheet music" feature. It only works if you enter the address of a music-recognition (OMR) server that you choose or host yourself. If you use it, the image or PDF you select is sent to that server you configured so it can be turned into playable notes. We do not operate that server, do not receive those files, and the app does not keep them after the result is returned. If you never enter a server address, nothing is ever uploaded.

Camera and files

If you choose to scan sheet music, the app may ask to use your camera or photo/file picker so you can capture or select a page. These images are used only for the scan you requested and are handled as described above. The app does not access your camera or files for any other purpose.

Children

Ninja Piano is a music-learning app suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly gather data from children.
